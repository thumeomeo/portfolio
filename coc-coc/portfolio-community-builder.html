01Why community

Communities don't grow from content. They grow from care.

I started as a teaching assistant for kindergarteners — where I learned to hold the attention of the world's toughest audience with language mini-games.

Then came business-English classes for Viettel Networks staff — busy professionals whose motivation needed re-warming every single week.

Most recently, at EASYIELTS (HOCMAI × GALAXY Education), I made dozens of consultation calls and heard the real worries of IELTS learners: lost basics, fear of Speaking, plateaued band scores. I saw the exact moment a learner decides to enroll — it's when they feel someone finally understands them.

That, to me, is the Community Builder job at ScoreX: creating a space where people ask without fear of judgment, have fun while actually learning, and trust the center before they ever buy a course.

03Ideas

Four things I'd love to test at ScoreX

Facebook Group

"No-judgment IELTS questions"

A pinned weekly thread for the most basic questions. Beginners' biggest fear is being laughed at — remove that fear and the community earns trust. Trust is step zero of conversion.

TikTok / Reels

"Guess the band — 15s Speaking"

Short clip: hear one Speaking answer, the community guesses the band, a ScoreX teacher reveals it with one tip. Repeatable format, shareable, funnels viewers into the group.

Zalo / Discord

"7 days, 7 collocations" check-in

A mini-challenge with a simple leaderboard; finishers get tagged in a weekend shout-out. A daily habit gives members a reason to open the group every day.

Whole ecosystem

"Real band-up stories"

Interviews with ScoreX learners who just hit their target — told in their own voice, no sugar-coating. Authentic social proof that matches ScoreX's "serious companionship" positioning.
